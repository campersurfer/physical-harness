#### 2. Key Technical Findings & Calculations
1. **Omission of Primary Consolidation Calculation in `CB-GEO-007` (Sheet 4):**
   - Although the EOR transcribed all Stratum 2 clay consolidation parameters ($H = 18\text{ ft}$, $C_c = 0.28$, $C_r = 0.045$, $e_0 = 0.92$, $\sigma'_0 = 1,850\text{ psf}$, $\sigma'_p = 1,900\text{ psf}$, $\Delta\sigma = 900\text{ psf}$) onto Sheets 2 and 5 of `CB-GEO-007`, **Sheet 4 (Consolidation)** leaves every calculation entry blank (`"—"`) with the note: *"Not computed. Serviceability taken from the immediate settlement result on Sheet 3."* On Sheet 3, the EOR evaluated only short-term ($t = 0.1\text{ yr}$, $C_2 = 1.00$) Schmertmann elastic settlement ($S_{i,\max} = 0.90\text{ in}$ at F-C6; $\Delta S_{i,\max} = 0.20\text{ in}$ diagonal / $0.08\text{ in}$ orthogonal) to claim compliance with the 1.0 in / 0.5 in limits.
2. **Primary Consolidation & Combined Total Settlement Exceeding Tolerance by >6×:**
   - Under $\sigma'_0 = 1,850\text{ psf}$ and $\Delta\sigma = 900\text{ psf}$ ($\sigma'_f = 2,750\text{ psf} > \sigma'_p = 1,900\text{ psf}$):
     - **Normally-Consolidated Formula (*KGI-2024-0417 Rev.2* §8.1):**
       $$S_c(\text{NC}) = \frac{C_c \cdot H}{1 + e_0}\log_{10}\left(\frac{\sigma'_0 + \Delta\sigma}{\sigma'_0}\right) = \frac{0.28 \times 216\text{ in}}{1.92}\log_{10}\left(\frac{2,750}{1,850}\right) = 31.500\text{ in} \times 0.172161 = \mathbf{5.42\text{ in}}\ (0.452\text{ ft})$$
     - **Exact Two-Branch Bilinear Formula ($\sigma'_0 \to \sigma'_p$ on $C_r$, $\sigma'_p \to \sigma'_f$ on $C_c$):**
       $$S_c(\text{2-branch}) = \frac{216\text{ in}}{1.92}\left[0.045\log_{10}\left(\frac{1,900}{1,850}\right) + 0.28\log_{10}\left(\frac{2,750}{1,900}\right)\right] = 0.059\text{ in} + 5.058\text{ in} = \mathbf{5.12\text{ in}}\ (0.426\text{ ft})$$
     - **Combined Long-Term Total Settlement ($S_{\text{total}} = S_i + S_c$):** Adding $S_i = 0.90\text{ in}$ yields **$S_{\text{total}} = 6.32\text{ in}$** (NC basis) or **$6.02\text{ in}$** (two-branch basis), exceeding the **1.0 in maximum total settlement limit** by **602% to 632%** ($S_c$ alone is **512% to 542%** of the limit).
3. **Long-Term Differential Settlement & Angular Distortion Exceeding Tolerance:**
   - Comparing interior footings ($\Delta\sigma = 900\text{ psf}$, $S_c = 5.12\text{–}5.42\text{ in}$, $S_i = 0.82\text{–}0.90\text{ in}$) against smaller $8.5\text{ ft} \times 8.5\text{ ft}$ corner/south-edge footings F-C12 and F-C11 (single-footing 2:1 $\Delta\sigma \approx 629\text{–}670\text{ psf}$ at mid-clay, $S_c = 3.70\text{–}4.23\text{ in}$, $S_i = 0.70\text{–}0.73\text{ in}$) yields a minimum 2:1 differential consolidation settlement of $\Delta S_c = 1.19\text{ to }1.42\text{ in}$ and combined $\Delta S_{\text{total}} = \mathbf{1.28\text{ to }1.62\text{ in}}$ (or **2.0 to 3.1 in** when accounting for Boussinesq multi-footing overlap / field differential-to-total ratios), exceeding the **0.5 in differential tolerance** by **256% to 622%** and violating the **1/500 angular distortion limit** ($0.58\text{ in}$ over a $24\text{ ft}$ bay).
4. **Time-Rate of Consolidation & Correlation with 06/26/2026 Monitoring (-0.61 in at MP-7):**
   - With $H_{\text{dr}} = 9.0\text{ ft}$ and $c_v = 0.040\text{–}0.042\text{ ft}^2/\text{day}$, primary consolidation requires **$t_{50} = 381\text{–}399\text{ days}$ ($1.04\text{–}1.09\text{ yr}$)** and **$t_{90} = 1,641\text{–}1,718\text{ days}$ ($4.49\text{–}4.70\text{ yr}$)**. Over the 196-day monitoring window (`12/12/2025` to `06/26/2026`), instantaneous $U(196\text{d}) = 35.1\%\text{–}35.9\%$ ($T_v = 0.097\text{–}0.101$) and ramped-load effective $U_{\text{eff}}(t/2 = 98\text{d}) \approx 25\%$ ($T_v \approx 0.050$). Because baseline was established after footings/pedestals were cast and only staged construction dead loads (~45%–55% of full $D+L$) were applied from 01/2026 to 06/2026, the observed **$-0.61\text{ in}$** at MP-7 (FTG-C7) closely matches theoretical early-stage primary consolidation.
   - Crucially, the 14-day settlement increment at MP-7 has **monotonically accelerated** from **$-0.03\text{ in}/14\text{d}$** (`-0.00214 in/day`) in Rounds 1–4 to **$-0.07\text{ in}/14\text{d}$** (`-0.00500 in/day`) in Round 15 (`06/12/2026 → 06/26/2026`), proving active virgin consolidation. At the current velocity of $-0.07\text{ in}/14\text{d}$, the remaining $0.39\text{ in}$ margin to the $1.0\text{ in}$ limit will be exhausted in **~78 days** (~`09/12/2026`). Moreover, observed differential settlement on `06/26/2026` (**$0.23\text{ in}$ over $24\text{ ft}$** orthogonal at MP-3/MP-7 and MP-8/MP-12; **$0.30\text{ in}$** diagonal at MP-7/MP-12) has **already exceeded** the EOR's total predicted differential settlement ($\Delta S_i = 0.20\text{ in}$ diagonal / $0.08\text{ in}$ orthogonal).
5. **Drawing and Calculation Discrepancies:**
   - *Sheet S-201 Rev.4* (`05/29/2026`), Detail `3/S-201` shows a typical interior Block C footing as **$7'\text{-}6" \times 7'\text{-}6" \times 2'\text{-}0"$** with underside at **El. 746.0 ft** (which under $412\text{–}432\text{ kips}$ would impose $7,324\text{–}7,680\text{ psf}$ vs. $4,000\text{ psf}$ allowable), contradicting `CB-GEO-007` Sheet 2 and as-built survey records (`10.5 ft × 10.5 ft × 36 in` interior footings; `CB-GEO-007` underside at **El. 740.5 ft**). Additionally, `CB-GEO-007` Sheet 3 models Stratum 1 sand modulus down to $z = 5.25\text{ ft}$ below footing base despite clay starting at $z = 2.5\text{ ft}$ below El. 740.5 ft, and lists C6 as heavier than C7 whereas field records designate MP-7 (C7) as the heaviest tributary column.
